## 3D Model: A Cultural Heritage {#3d-model}

The model used in this project is the [Winged Victory of Samothrace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winged_Victory_of_Samothrace) (The Louvre, Paris). 

It was provided by [Scan The World](https://www.myminifactory.com/users/Scan%20The%20World), an open-source initiative dedicated to sharing 3D printable cultural artifacts.  

You can download the specific model used for this simulation on [MyMiniFactory](https://www.myminifactory.com/object/3d-print-winged-victory-of-samothrace-2073).
